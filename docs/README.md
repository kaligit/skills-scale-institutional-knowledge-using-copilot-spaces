# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the project management guidance used by OctoAcme to move from idea to delivery. The process begins with initiation, where the team validates the business problem, identifies stakeholders, defines success criteria, and creates a lightweight one-pager with goals, risks, and rough resource needs. Once the initiative is approved, the team moves into planning, turning the idea into a prioritized backlog, milestone plan, and definition of done. These steps make the work actionable and help align the broader organization before execution begins.

The team’s operating model is structured around defined roles and recurring communication. Product leaders define outcomes and priorities, project managers coordinate schedules, risks, and status reporting, developers build and test solutions, QA validates quality and acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input and approval. OctoAcme’s principles emphasize customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decision-making, and psychological safety so teams can collaborate effectively while learning quickly. Communication is deliberately regular through standups, sprint or milestone reviews, weekly syncs, and escalation paths that surface blockers early and escalate when necessary.

Quality is treated as a continuous responsibility rather than a final checkpoint. Work is managed through project boards, pull requests, CI testing, security scanning, and acceptance criteria checks, all designed to reduce risk and improve traceability. The lifecycle also includes risk management, stakeholder updates, release planning, rollback readiness, and post-deploy verification. When a release or milestone completes, the team captures lessons learned through retrospectives and converts them into action items with owners and due dates so improvement becomes part of the ongoing process.

## Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a simple but disciplined project management lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. The goal is to keep projects aligned to customer value while maintaining clarity on ownership, communication, and quality expectations. This documentation set provides the operational guidance needed to run projects consistently across teams and milestones.
