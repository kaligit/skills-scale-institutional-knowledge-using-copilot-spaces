# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers turn product and project priorities into achievable delivery plans. They balance team capacity, technical execution, and cross-functional coordination to keep projects moving.

### Responsibilities
- Translate roadmap priorities into delivery plans and sprint commitments
- Manage team capacity, dependencies, and resourcing needs
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on sequencing and risk mitigation
- Support engineering quality, technical decision-making, and delivery health
- Coordinate escalations across teams when dependencies or blockers emerge

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, sustainable execution
- Reduce delivery risk and unblock teams quickly
- Maintain a healthy engineering rhythm and clear ownership

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering planning and delivery updates
- Dependency and risk discussions with PM and Product leads
- Technical tradeoff conversations with developers and stakeholders

---

## Designers / UX Leads

### Role Summary
Designers and UX Leads ensure the solution is usable, accessible, and aligned to customer needs. They help turn requirements into experiences that are both valuable and understandable.

### Responsibilities
- Define or refine user experience direction and design principles
- Participate in discovery, ideation, and validation activities
- Review feature flows for usability, accessibility, and clarity
- Collaborate with Product and Engineering on trade-offs and prioritization
- Validate that the implemented experience matches the intended user outcomes

### Goals
- Improve user experience and overall product usability
- Ensure customer needs are reflected in delivery decisions
- Reduce rework caused by unclear product or experience expectations

### Typical Communication
- Product and design reviews
- Usability testing and feedback loops
- Design handoff discussions with engineering teams

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads help protect the product, systems, and organizational obligations by identifying and mitigating risk across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Identify project risks and required controls or approvals
- Coordinate security reviews, testing, and remediation
- Support release readiness and risk assessment for sensitive changes
- Partner with stakeholders on governance and operational safeguards

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure and improve trust in project delivery
- Ensure compliance with required standards and policies
- Support secure, resilient implementation and release practices

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk assessments
- Release readiness checks and exception approvals
- Escalation paths for vulnerabilities or compliance concerns

---

## Support / Customer Success Leads

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success Leads connect project delivery to real customer impact by surfacing feedback, issues, and service needs.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback and issue trends from support channels
- Prioritize operational pain points and improvement opportunities
- Share service-impacting risks with project teams and stakeholders
- Help validate whether product changes improve customer outcomes
- Coordinate handoff of issues and product feedback to the relevant owners

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure delivery decisions reflect operational realities
- Close gaps between product improvements and customer needs

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and issue triage
- Stakeholder updates on support trends and risk areas
- Collaboration with PM, Product, and Engineering on prioritization

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define measurement plans and help teams assess whether project outcomes are being achieved.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and measurement frameworks
- Create or maintain dashboards and reporting for key outcomes
- Support experimentation, analysis, and decision-making
- Help connect product performance to business and customer impact
- Provide evidence for prioritization and optimization decisions

### Goals
- Improve visibility into product and project outcomes
- Support data-informed decision-making
- Measure impact and guide iterative improvements

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and metric definitions
- Performance reporting to PM, Product, and stakeholders
- Analysis sessions to inform trade-offs and roadmap adjustments

---

## Platform / DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Platform and DevOps Engineers provide the technical foundation for reliable delivery, automation, and operational resilience.

### Responsibilities
- Support deployment pipelines, environments, and uptime
- Improve automation for build, test, release, and rollback processes
- Help ensure infrastructure reliability, observability, and recovery
- Partner with Engineering on release readiness and production stability
- Identify operational risks and improve team delivery efficiency

### Goals
- Increase delivery speed without sacrificing reliability
- Improve operational consistency and recovery capabilities
- Reduce friction in deployment and release workflows

### Typical Communication
- Deployment and infrastructure planning
- Release reviews and rollback readiness checks
- Operational incident coordination with engineering and project leads

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.

### How the expanded roles interact with existing ones

- Project Managers and Product Managers maintain overall project alignment, scope, and stakeholder communications while specialist roles provide deeper execution, quality, and operational expertise.
- Engineering Managers work closely with Developers to translate priorities into delivery plans, coordinate dependencies, and support technical execution.
- Designers and UX Leads collaborate with Product and Engineering to validate usability and customer needs.
- Security / Compliance Leads coordinate with QA and engineering teams to reduce risk and confirm required safeguards.
- Support / Customer Success Leads feed customer and operational signals back into the product and delivery process.
- Data / Analytics Leads provide evidence for success measurement and prioritization decisions.
- Platform / DevOps Engineers support Engineers and Release stakeholders to keep delivery reliable and repeatable.
- Stakeholders remain accountable for approvals and strategic direction, while these specialists provide the necessary expertise to make informed delivery decisions.
